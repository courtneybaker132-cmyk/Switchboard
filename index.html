Focus: 25 minutes, then a 5-minute break. Breathing: 2 minutes, with 4 seconds in and 6 seconds out. Breathe comfortably at your own pace. Choosing a mode replaces the current session.

Tap the board for Pause / Resume. Timers keep elapsed time if the tablet sleeps, but cannot wake it or guarantee a sound while asleep. Reloading ends the session.

Appearance

Changes the board's appearance, not the tablet backlight. Night mode dims it further from 10 PM to 6 AM. Settings stay readable.

Color scenes last 12 seconds after each regular five-scene cycle. Seasonal palettes follow the calendar month. Ambient mode uses rainbow when this option is Off.

Sound

Flap sounds are softer after 5 PM, and regular sounds and focus chimes are off from 10 PM to 6 AM. An enabled alarm can still sound during quiet hours. Tap Fullscreen / sound after reloading. Breathing and ambient scenes stay quiet.
